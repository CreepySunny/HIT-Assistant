# Potential Societal Impact Assessment: Hyper Intelligent Trading Assistant

## Impact on Society

I developed the Hyper Intelligent Trading Assistant as an internal AI tool for professional baseball teams to enhance decision-making in player trades. By leveraging the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the assistant securely connects to the team’s internal databases and analytics tools, ensuring that recommendations are always based on the most current and relevant data. This integration not only streamlines the decision process but also helps reduce human bias, identify creative trade opportunities, and improve the fairness and quality of trades. Used responsibly, the tool can contribute to greater competitive balance and transparency in the league.

However, the adoption of advanced AI tools is not without risks. If only some teams have access to such technology, competitive inequalities could worsen, and over-reliance on algorithmic suggestions may erode the human element of the sport. To address these concerns, I advocate for league-wide best practices, transparency, and always keeping humans in the loop for all major decisions. The goal is to ensure that AI enhances, rather than replaces, the human judgment and tradition that define the sport.

## Stakeholders

The main stakeholders are team general managers and analysts, who use the assistant to inform their decisions, as well as scouting and analytics staff who provide the underlying data. Owners and executives are also key, as they invest in and oversee the outcomes of AI-driven decisions. Professional players, while not direct users, are affected by the trades and decisions the assistant helps facilitate. I engage these groups through consultation, training, and transparent communication, ensuring that the system is fair and that human judgment is always part of the process.

It is important to recognize that players’ careers and livelihoods can be impacted by AI-informed trades. Therefore, I maintain a human-in-the-loop approach, where coaches and managers can factor in leadership, team chemistry, and personal circumstances before finalizing any trade. This approach helps balance the efficiency of AI with the empathy and context that only humans can provide.

## Privacy

The assistant processes personal data about players, such as performance statistics, health information, and contract details, but only uses information relevant to player evaluation. All data remains internal and secure via MCP, with strict access controls and encryption. I comply with GDPR and the EU AI Act, conducting privacy impact assessments and ensuring that no decisions are made solely by AI. Regular audits and updates to privacy practices are planned as the system evolves, and I am committed to transparency with players if decisions significantly affect them.

As the assistant evolves, I remain vigilant about privacy risks, especially if new types of data (such as biometric or psychological metrics) are considered. Before incorporating any new data, I evaluate privacy impacts and obtain necessary consents. My approach is to proactively address privacy, protect individuals’ rights, and uphold the ethical standards expected in professional sports organizations.

## Data

The effectiveness of the assistant depends on the quality of internal scouting data and statistics. I recognize the subjectivity and limitations of both, so I have implemented bias testing and continuous improvement processes. The system is updated with new data each season, and feedback from users is used to refine the model. Data governance includes version control, transparency about data sources, and regular checks for bias or errors.

I also maintain a feedback loop from human decisions to refine the assistant’s suggestions. If a trade is rejected for reasons the AI did not consider, that feedback is logged and used to improve future recommendations. This process ensures that the assistant remains accurate, fair, and relevant as the game and its data evolve.

## Transparency

I provide clear documentation and explanations for users, including the rationale behind each AI suggestion. The system maintains logs for audit purposes, and users can question or challenge outputs. While trade deliberations are internal, I am prepared to explain AI involvement to players or regulators if required. I am also developing improved explanation features and audit reports to further build trust and compliance.

Transparency is not just about explaining how the AI works, but also about acknowledging its limitations. Users are informed of scenarios where the assistant might not perform well, and I encourage a culture where human judgment and critical thinking are valued alongside AI insights.

## Inclusivity

The tool is available to all relevant team staff, with training provided to ensure accessibility regardless of technical background. While not all teams have access yet, I expect adoption to grow as the benefits become clear. The model is designed to avoid bias, and development involved a diverse group of experts. I conduct fairness audits and encourage knowledge-sharing to ensure the benefits of AI are widely distributed.

Inclusivity also means ensuring that the assistant’s recommendations are fair and unbiased toward all individuals. I have taken steps to prevent any built-in bias, and I regularly test the model’s outputs to ensure no group is systematically disadvantaged. This commitment to fairness is central to the responsible use of AI in sports.

## Sustainability

I minimize the environmental impact of the assistant by using efficient cloud resources, optimizing model usage, and leveraging MCP to avoid redundant infrastructure. The tool is software-focused, with updates rather than hardware replacements. I monitor energy use and partner with eco-friendly providers, aiming to reduce the system’s footprint over time.

Sustainability also means ensuring the tool remains relevant and effective as technology and the sport evolve. I plan for regular updates, continuous monitoring, and the adoption of best practices to keep the assistant both environmentally and operationally sustainable.

## Hateful and Criminal Actors

Strong cybersecurity measures protect the system from hacking or misuse. Access is tightly controlled, and all activity is monitored for anomalies. The tool is designed to comply with league rules and employment laws, and I enforce strict ethical use policies among users. Regular security audits and updates are part of ongoing risk management.

I am also aware of the risks of insider misuse or data leaks, and I have implemented protocols to detect and respond to such threats. By maintaining a strong security posture, I ensure that the assistant remains a tool for good within the bounds of law and sportsmanship.

## Human Values

Human decision-makers retain control, with the AI serving as an advisor. The tool reduces workload and stress for staff, but safeguards are in place to prevent over-reliance. I ensure players are treated as individuals, not just data points, and encourage a culture that values both innovation and tradition. Ethical alignment is maintained through ongoing consultation and training.

I also foster an environment where users are encouraged to question the AI’s outputs and to bring their own expertise and values to the decision-making process. This balance between technology and human values is essential for the responsible use of AI in sports.

## Future

Looking ahead, I see both positive and negative scenarios. Ideally, ethical AI adoption will lead to smarter, fairer trades and improved experiences for all stakeholders. Risks include over-automation, loss of human judgment, or misuse if the technology is acquired by less ethical parties. I plan for these possibilities by advocating for strong governance, transparency, and continual ethical review.

By remaining vigilant and proactive, I aim to ensure that the Hyper Intelligent Trading Assistant continues to be a force for good in my organization and the wider sports community.
